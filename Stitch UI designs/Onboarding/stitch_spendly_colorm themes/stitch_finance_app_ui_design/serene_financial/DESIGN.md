## Brand & Style

This design system replaces anxiety-inducing neon greens, aggressive dark modes, and high-frequency analytical clutter with an atmosphere of quiet competence and composed wealth management. It blends modern editorial minimalism with organic, tactile warmth to project reassurance, stability, and enduring trust.

Targeted at discerning individuals navigating personal wealth, investments, and daily financial stewardship, the interface cultivates a sense of unhurried clarity. The visual language rejects gamified trading tropes in favor of soft natural daylight, quiet botanical tones, deep slate charcoals, and gentle ambient depth. Every interaction feels anchored, deliberate, and respectful of cognitive bandwidth.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical flora, sun-warmed linen, and deep geological slate:

- **Primary (`#40916C`) & Secondary (`#52B788`)**: Deep sage and botanical eucalyptus greens replace harsh fluorescent neons. Used for primary balance affirmations, positive portfolio trajectories, and key conversion paths.
- **Warm Ambers (`#E9C46A` / `#F4A261`)**: Honeyed gold and sunlit amber serve as cautionary signifiers, pending transaction markers, and informational highlights without inciting alarm.
- **Terracotta Rose (`#E76F51`)**: A softened brick earth tone that delivers negative balance, outflow, and alert signals with humanized restraint rather than harsh system panic.
- **Warm Charcoal & Deep Slate (`#1E2522` / `#2D3732`)**: Used for high-emphasis typography and grounding structural accents, eliminating the sterile harshness of pure black.
- **Foundational Surfaces**: Soft alabaster (`#FBFBF7`), warm muted cream (`#F4F3EE`), and light sage tint (`#EBF0EC`) provide a cozy, paper-like canvas that softens digital fatigue.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between editorial intimacy and structured financial precision. 

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Brings warm, literary gravitas to overarching portfolio statements, monthly summaries, and hero balance amounts. It grounds monetary metrics in human scale, removing the volatile ticker sensation.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`)**: Provides geometric clarity, immaculate tabular digit rendering, and high legibility across transactional ledger feeds, metric cards, and interactive controls.
- **Numeric Alignment**: All balance tallies and monetary tables must activate proportional lining figures for editorial titles and tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within data columns and balance lists to guarantee seamless vertical scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous breathing room to foster composure and deliberateness. 

- **Desktop & Widescreen**: 12-column layout conforming to an inner max-width of 1280px. High-level net wealth cards, insight widgets, and transactional ledgers span balanced 4-, 6-, and 8-column arrangements.
- **Tablet**: 8-column layout with 24px gutters, collapsing secondary analytical sidebars beneath primary asset summaries.
- **Mobile**: Single-column vertical flow with 16px horizontal margins. Complex multi-column data collapses into layered cards and sequential thumb-friendly lists.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: White space is treated as an active calming agent. Never compress margins to fit more rows onto a single screen; lean into rhythmic vertical padding to minimize visual stress.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and stark cutouts, relying instead on warm tonal layering and soft, daylight-inspired ambient glows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base background rests at `#FBFBF7` (Soft Linen). Base card containers elevate to `#FFFFFF` with a 0.5px border tinted to `rgba(30, 37, 34, 0.06)`. Higher-priority interactive floating layers lift to warm muted surfaces (`#F4F3EE`).
- **Warm Ambient Glows**: Resting components utilize diffused, low-opacity shadows tinted with deep slate and warm sage:
  - *Resting card*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 55, 50, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(64, 145, 108, 0.04)`
  - *Elevated state / Dropdown*: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 55, 50, 0.06), 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 55, 50, 0.08)`
  - *Botanical Focus / Halo*: `0 0 0 3px rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.22)`
- **Tactile Micro-Textures**: Surfaces may incorporate a 1% SVG monochromatic stipple grain, mimicking premium fine-grain archival paper to impart physical presence and organic weight.

## Shapes

The design system employs refined, welcoming geometry with level `2` roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline). 

- **Small Interactive Targets** (buttons, badges, inputs): 8px to 10px corner radius.
- **Medium Panels & Cards** (`rounded-lg`): 16px radius, offering a softened frame that eliminates sharp visual edges.
- **Major Modals & Sheet Surfaces** (`rounded-xl`): 24px radius, creating soft tactile envelopes for sensitive transactions.
- **Pill Accents**: Restricted exclusively to status tags, filter chips, and pill badges to preserve crisp architectural boundaries on functional modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep botanical green (`#40916C`) fill, crisp white Manrope text, subtle inset highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, and soft sage ambient shadow. On hover, shifts softly to `#357A5B`.
- **Secondary**: Tinted sage linen surface (`#EBF0EC`), deep slate text (`#1E2522`), and zero drop shadow. On hover, deepens to `#DFE7E1`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background with gentle slate text; gains `#F4F3EE` fill on hover.

### Status Indicators & Chips
- **Success / Positive Growth**: Soft sage background (`rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.14)`), deep eucalyptus label (`#2D6A4F`), accompanied by a solid 6px indicator dot (`#52B788`).
- **Pending / In Review**: Warm honey amber background (`rgba(233, 196, 106, 0.18)`), deep umber amber label (`#9C6B15`), paired with a warm amber dot (`#E9C46A`).
- **Attention / Outflow**: Soft terracotta background (`rgba(231, 111, 81, 0.12)`), muted rust label (`#A33B20`), paired with an earthen terracotta dot (`#E76F51`).

### Cards & Ledger Lists
- **Asset Cards**: Clean white surfaces edged by faint sage-slate borders. Features large Newsreader balances balanced above Manrope sub-metrics and micro-sparklines in soothing eucalyptus monochrome.
- **Transaction Lists**: Alternating borderless rows cushioned with `space-sm` padding. Merchant iconography uses circular badges tinted in muted neutrals (`#F4F3EE`). Outflows appear in deep slate (`#1E2522`); inflows feature `#40916C` preceded by a discreet `+`.

### Inputs & Selection
- **Input Fields**: Soft linen backgrounds (`#FBFBF7`) framed with 1px neutral borders (`rgba(30, 37, 34, 0.12)`). Focused inputs animate a gentle sage halo (`rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.25)`) and brighten the surface to pure white.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 8px rounded squares and circles with warm slate borders when inactive. Active state fills with `#40916C` bearing an ivory checkmark.

### Financial Summary Module (System-Specific)
- **Net Wealth Banner**: Warm slate-tinted canvas (`#2D3732`) with soft golden-sage radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(82, 183, 136, 0.12), transparent 60%)`). Features high-contrast ivory Newsreader numerals, radiating calm authority without blinding brightness.
